Twilight

Although considered an excellent leader by many, she often doubts her own abilities. Twilight is a Nightwing-Rainwing-Seawing hybrid. She's that one overpowered Wings of Fire OC that every fan creates at some point in their lives.

She's a student at Jade Mountain Academy, leader of the Misty Winglet with her peers Lunarmoon, Snowsheet and Beryl.

Twilight is easily overwhelmed by her mind-reading gift, therefore she chooses to not read minds. Occasionally, she is able to get glimpses of the future.

Before enrolling in Jade Mountain Academy, she lived a quiet life in the rainforest with her mother Moonlight and her sister Shadow.
