Kiro

He's been alone his entire life, and had no one to smile for. Due to this, he's always seen with a neutral expression, as there is quite literally no reason for him to act.

When he does smile though, it is genuine and true. Kiro loves company, although he absolutely refuses to admit it. He will do kind things in secret for other people to make their day.

Kiro is the literal embodiment of "be yourself and don't care what other people think". He's only ever happy for himself, which is why he often comes off as uncaring, and this has landed him in a lonely situation.
